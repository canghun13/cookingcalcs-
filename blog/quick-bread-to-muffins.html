Banana Bread to Muffins (and Back): How Many, What Temperature, How Long

November 2026 · 6 min read

One 9×5 loaf recipe makes about 12 standard muffins. Raise the oven 25–50°F above the loaf temperature and bake about 23–28 minutes at 375°F (or 20–25 at 400°F), instead of the hour a loaf takes at 350°F. Going the other way, 12 muffins' worth of batter fills one 9×5 pan: bake at 350°F for 45–60 minutes.

Banana bread, zucchini bread, pumpkin bread and most other quick breads are muffin batter in a loaf pan, so they move between the two shapes without changing a single ingredient. What changes is the oven temperature and the time. A loaf is thick and needs a moderate oven so the middle sets before the crust gets too dark; muffins are small and do best in a hotter oven that gives them a domed top before they dry out.

Loaf to Muffins, Step by Step

  1. Heat the oven 25–50°F hotter than the loaf recipe says — usually 375°F, or 400°F for a stronger dome.
  2. Grease or line a 12-cup standard muffin pan. A standard cup holds about ½ cup, so fill it three-quarters full: about ⅓ cup of batter. A #12 portion scoop is exactly ⅓ cup; see portion scoop sizes.
  3. Bake 23–28 minutes at 375°F (20–25 at 400°F) until the tops spring back and a toothpick in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  4. Cool in the pan 5 minutes, then lift the muffins out so the bottoms don't steam.

If the loaf recipe has a crumb or streusel topping, use about half as much per muffin as you would picture — twelve tops have more surface than one loaf, so the full amount usually covers them all.

Muffins to a Loaf

Pour 12 standard muffins' worth of batter into a greased 9×5-inch pan, no more than three-quarters full. Lower the oven 25–50°F from the muffin recipe, usually to 350°F, and bake 45–60 minutes, checking at 45. If the top browns before the center sets, lay a sheet of foil loosely over it for the last 15 minutes. An 8½×4½-inch pan holds less, so the batter sits deeper and the loaf takes a little longer; leave out a few spoonfuls and bake them as a couple of muffins rather than overfill it. For any other pan, the baking pan size converter works out the scaling from pan volumes.

Mini and Jumbo Muffins From a Standard Recipe

A recipe for 12 standard muffins makes about 36–40 minis or about 6 jumbos. Minis bake at 350°F for 12–14 minutes with no high-heat start; they dry out quickly, so pull them as soon as a toothpick comes out clean. Jumbos start at 425°F for 5 minutes to set the dome, then finish at 350°F for 22–25 minutes so the centers cook through without burning the outside.

Which Recipes Convert Best

  • Converts cleanly: banana, zucchini, pumpkin, cranberry-orange, lemon-poppy seed, cornbread, most muffin recipes.
  • Watch closely: very wet batters with a lot of fruit or a swirl of cream cheese — they can sink in the middle as a loaf, so give them the full time and check the center.
  • Yeasted breads are different: a sandwich loaf or brioche dough is not a quick bread, and rolls follow their own timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many muffins does a banana bread recipe make?

A recipe for one 9×5-inch loaf makes about 12 standard muffins, 36 mini muffins or 6 jumbo muffins, filling the cups about three-quarters full.

What temperature and time for banana bread muffins?

Raise the oven 25–50°F above the loaf temperature. At 375°F, standard muffins take about 23–28 minutes; at 400°F, about 20–25 minutes. A 9×5 loaf at 350°F takes roughly an hour.

Can I bake muffin batter as a loaf?

Yes. Twelve standard muffins' worth of batter fills one 9×5-inch loaf pan. Lower the oven to 350°F and bake about 45–60 minutes, checking at 45 minutes.

How many mini muffins does a regular muffin recipe make?

About three times as many: a recipe for 12 standard muffins makes roughly 36 to 40 minis. Bake them at 350°F for 12–14 minutes.
