    How We Get Our Numbers

    A calculator is only as good as the figures behind it.

    Published sources first

    Safe temperatures follow USDA food safety guidance. Scoop sizes and produce yields come from the USDA Food Buying Guide and industry yield tables; pan capacities from manufacturer specifications. Where sources disagree, the page says so and shows the range.

    The working is on the page

    Tool pages explain the formula behind the answer and include worked examples, so you can check a result by hand — or adapt it when your situation doesn't match the defaults.

    One number, everywhere

    A stick of butter is 113 g on every page that mentions it. When we add a page, its figures are checked against the existing charts and calculators so the site doesn't contradict itself.

    Corrections welcome

    If a number looks wrong for your kitchen, email contact@cookingcalcs.com. More about the site is on the About page.
