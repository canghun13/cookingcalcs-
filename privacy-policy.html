Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CookingCalcs ("we", "us", or "our") operates cookingcalcs.com. This page explains what data is collected when you visit the site, how it is used, which third parties are involved, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not request or store your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information in order for you to use the tools on this site. Any values you enter into a calculator — weights, temperatures, prices, ingredient amounts — are processed in your browser and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Like most websites, we do collect limited technical and usage information automatically, described in the sections below.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, approximate geographic region, and what type of device and browser is used. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors. Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish between sessions and returning visitors.

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. We use Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.
  • For more detail on how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, see Google's policy page.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks may also use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in the advertisements that appear on this site. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and to personalize the advertising content you see. We do not have access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. On this site they are used for analytics and advertising as described above. You can refuse or delete cookies through your browser settings; the calculators and reference content on this site will continue to work normally if you do, though ads you see may become less relevant.

Visitors from the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, applicable law gives you rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Where consent is required for advertising or analytics cookies, it is requested before those cookies are set, and you may withdraw it at any time through your browser settings or the opt-out links above.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

External Links

Some pages link to external websites for reference, such as USDA food-safety guidance. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or about how your data is handled, please reach us via the contact page.
